All in harmony again.

Remember the one universal remote that finally ran the whole living room? It got discontinued. The servers went dark, and a drawer full of hardware went dark with them.

reMaestro brings the one-remote dream back, and this time it lives on your hardware. Nobody can switch it off but you.

It runs on your hardware

Flash a Raspberry Pi, plug it into the rack, and it's yours. Your devices, your data, your network. Nothing phones home, nothing needs a subscription, nothing disappears when a company sunsets an app.

  • No cloud. Everything stays on your LAN. It works when your internet doesn't.
  • An appliance, not a project. Self-updating, self-healing, over-the-air releases.
  • Yours to bend. Open drivers, custom remotes, rules and activities, no ceiling.
